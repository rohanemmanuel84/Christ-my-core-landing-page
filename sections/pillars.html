THE FIVE PILLARS

Faith isn't one thing. It's five living dimensions.

Every follower of Christ grows — or stagnates — across five essential areas. ChristMyCore helps you see which ones need water.

🕊️
Abiding
Your connection with God — prayer, scripture, surrender
🌱
Discipling
How you pour into others and multiply your faith
🔒
Private Life
Who you are when no one is watching
🤝
Relationships
Love in action — family, community, the hard conversations
👑
Kingdom Building
How you extend the reign of Christ in the world
