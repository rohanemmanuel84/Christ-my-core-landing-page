THE FIVE PILLARS

Faith isn't one thing. It's five living dimensions.

Every follower of Christ grows — or stagnates — across five essential areas. ChristMyCore helps you see which ones need water.

🕊️
Ignite
Keeping the spiritual fire and personal walk burning brightly.
🌱
Insight
Gaining theological depth and foundational understanding.
🔒
Upright
Maintaining stellar character and stewardship.
🤝
Unite
Building bridges, fellowship, and healthy engagements.
👑
Invite
Bringing others into the Kingdom and living out the Great Commission.
